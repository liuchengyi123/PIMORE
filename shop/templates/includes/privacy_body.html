This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use the Pimo website, mobile site, or native apps (Android / iOS).

1. Who we are

Pimo is a cross-border household / small-goods storefront. For privacy questions, use in-app / on-site customer chat, or email support@linkiip.com.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you shop, we may process: - Account & contact: email, display name, sign-in provider (Apple / Google / Facebook / X), hashed credentials if you use email login. - Orders & shipping: shipping name, address, phone, order contents, payment status, tracking identifiers. - Payments: we do not store full card numbers. Card payments are handled by payment processors (e.g. Stripe / PayPal / LianLian). We may keep brand + last 4 digits and processor tokens needed for refunds. - Device & usage: IP-based country / currency, language preference, app version, basic diagnostics, cookies or similar IDs needed for login, cart, fraud prevention, and analytics. - Support: messages you send to customer service, including optional product / order context. - Marketing (optional): invite / share links and campaign attribution when you use those features.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Why we use data
  • Provide the storefront, cart, checkout, delivery, returns, and customer support.
  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and payment risk.
  • Localize language UI and price currency (currency follows IP / location; language is your choice).
  • Improve product quality and fix crashes.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.
4. Legal bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

Contract performance, legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement), consent where required (e.g. certain cookies / marketing), and legal obligation.

5. Sharing

We share data only with processors that help run the service, under instructions, such as: - hosting / CDN / email / analytics vendors, - payment processors, - logistics / fulfillment partners, - social login providers you choose (Apple, Google, Meta/Facebook, X).

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.

6. International transfers

Orders are cross-border. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g. contractual clauses).

7. Retention

We keep account, order, and support records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle returns/chargebacks, meet tax/bookkeeping rules, and resolve disputes, then delete or anonymize when no longer required.

8. Your rights

Subject to local law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and withdraw consent. Contact us via chat or support@linkiip.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data authority.

9. Children

Pimo is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Account deletion

In the app or site, open Me → Help → Contact / Chat and request account deletion, or email support@linkiip.com from the address on the account. We will delete or anonymize personal data that is not required for legal retention (e.g. completed orders for tax).

11. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and processor safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Effective date” will change; continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes may also be highlighted in the app or on the site.

13. Contact
